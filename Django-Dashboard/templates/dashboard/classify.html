How it works

The text is cleaned (URLs, mentions, hashtags and non-alphabetic characters removed, lowercased) and passed through the trained PySpark MLlib pipeline: Tokenizer → StopWordsRemover → CountVectorizer → LogisticRegression. The model returns one of four classes: Negative, Positive, Neutral or Irrelevant.
